The Simple Version

ClipKeys processes everything 100% locally on your device. Nothing you type is ever sent anywhere. Ever.

What We Access

Keystrokes and clipboard contents — only to provide keyboard functionality. All data stays on your device only.

What We Never Do

We never send keystrokes, passwords, or clipboard contents to any server. We never log what you type. We never show ads or share data with anyone.

In-App Purchases

ClipKeys Pro is purchased through Google Play. Billing is handled entirely by Google. We receive only purchase confirmation — never payment details.
